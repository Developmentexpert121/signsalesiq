## Subscription Plans & Payment Gateway Flow

**Plans:** Basic, Standard, Premium

### 1. Subscription Gate (Super Admin Control)

There will be a toggle option in the **Super Admin Dashboard** called:

**Subscription Gate**
*(When enabled, admins must subscribe and pay before accessing any features.)*

---

## When Subscription Gate is ENABLED

### For Existing Admins:

* If an admin account was already created before the gate was enabled:

  * They will see a **notification reminder** to purchase a subscription plan.
  * Until they purchase a plan, reminders will continue.
  * Once the admin buys a plan, the popup/notification will stop.
  * After purchase, they will have full access according to their selected plan.

---

### For New Admins:

* If a new admin account is created while the Subscription Gate is active:

  * After login, the admin will be redirected only to the **Subscription Plans page**.
  * They will not be able to access any other dashboard pages.
  * Only two options will be available:

    * Subscribe to a plan
    * Logout
  * Once a plan is purchased, full dashboard access will be granted.

---

## When Subscription Gate is DISABLED

* Admins who already have an active subscription:

  * Their subscription will continue normally until the end of their billing cycle.
  * Monthly billing will proceed as per their selected plan.

* Admins who have not purchased any plan:

  * They will automatically get free access.
  * No subscription will be required.
