# SignSalesIQ - Owner Admin Manual

## Role Overview

As an Owner Admin, you manage your company's SignSalesIQ account. You can set up your company branding, manage your team of users, customize product rules, manage your subscription, create opportunities, and generate AI-powered sign mockups. You see all opportunities across your entire team.

---

## First-Time Login: Onboarding

When you first log in as an Owner Admin, you will be guided through a one-time setup wizard before accessing the main application.

Screen: The onboarding page shows the SignSalesIQ logo at the top, a "Welcome! Let's set up your account" heading, and a form with multiple sections.

### Onboarding Steps

1. Business Information
   - Business Address (required) - Your company's street address.
   - Phone Number (required) - Main business phone.
   - Email Address (required) - Primary contact email.
   - Website (optional) - Your company website URL.

2. Company Logo
   - Click the upload area or drag a file to upload your company logo.
   - Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, SVG, or PDF. SVG and PDF files are automatically converted to PNG.
   - The logo appears on your PDF proposals and may be used in AI mockup generation.

3. PDF Proposal Settings
   - PDF Template (optional): Upload a custom 8.5" x 11" PDF background for branded proposals. A "Download Sample Template" link is provided for reference.
   - Header Text (optional): Custom text that appears at the top of every proposal page.
   - Footer Text (optional): Custom text that appears at the bottom of every proposal page.
   - Banner Text (optional): Custom promotional text.
   - Primary Color and Accent Color: Customize the color scheme used in your PDF proposals.

4. Click "Complete Setup" to finish onboarding.

After completing onboarding, you will be taken to the Dashboard and can begin using the platform.

## Dashboard

After logging in (or after completing onboarding), you see the Dashboard.

Screen Layout:
- Top bar: Sidebar toggle on the left, "SignSalesIQ" text, dark mode toggle on the right.
- Left sidebar: Navigation links, Team Management section, your profile at the bottom.

### Stat Cards
- Total: Count of all opportunities across your team.

- Lost: Opportunities marked as lost.

### Recent Opportunities
- Shows the 5 most recent opportunities from your entire team (not just yours).
- Each card has a colored left border: green=Open, blue=Won, purple=Follow-up, red=Lost.
- Click any card to view details.

## User Management

Navigate to: Sidebar > Users

Screen Layout:
- Page title with your company name: "Manage users for [Company Name]"
- "Add User" button (top right).
- List of user cards below.

Important: You must complete the onboarding wizard before you can add users.

### User List
Each user card shows:
- Name and email.
- Role badge (color-coded).
- Edit and Delete action buttons.

### Adding a Team Member
1. Click "Add User".
2. Fill in the dialog:
   - Full Name (required).
   - Email (required) - This is their login username.
   - Password (required) - Their initial password. Use the eye icon to verify what you type.
   - Role - Choose from:
     - Admin - Can manage the team and company settings.
     - Sales - Creates and manages their own opportunities.
     - Account Manager - Same access as Sales.
     - Project Manager - Same access as Sales.
     - Outside Sales - Same access as Sales.
     - Inside Sales - Same access as Sales.
     - Sales Manager - Same access as Sales.
     - Designer - Same access as Sales.
     - Production Manager - Same access as Sales.
3. Click "Create User".
4. Share the login credentials with your team member.

### Key Difference: Admin vs Other Roles
- You (Admin) see ALL opportunities across your entire team.
- Sales and other roles see ONLY their own opportunities.
- This means you can monitor your team's progress and help with any opportunity.

### Editing a User
1. Click "Edit" on the user card.
2. Update name, email, or role.
3. Leave password blank to keep the existing password, or enter a new password to reset it.
4. Click "Save Changes".

### Deleting a User
1. Click "Delete" on the user card.
2. Confirm the deletion.
3. Note: Their opportunities will remain in the system.

## Rules Engine (Custom)

Navigate to: Sidebar > Rules Engine

Screen Layout:
- Page title: "Product Rules Engine"
- Two sections: "Your Custom Rules" and "Global Templates" (read-only).
- "Add Rule" button for creating custom rules.
- "Copy" button on global templates to duplicate into your custom rules.

### Understanding Rules
Rules determine what products are presented in each tier (Good/Better/Best) when a user generates mockups. Rules are matched by:
- Sign Type (e.g., Channel Letters)
- Location Type (Interior/Exterior/Vehicle)
- Budget Range (e.g., $2K-5K)

### Your Custom Rules
- These override the global templates set by the Super Admin.
- When one of your users generates mockups, the system looks for your custom rules first.
- If no custom rule matches, it falls back to the global template.

### Copying a Global Template
1. Scroll to the "Global Templates" section.
2. Find a rule you want to customize.
3. Click "Copy" on that rule.
4. The rule is copied into your custom rules section.
5. Click "Edit" to modify the copy for your organization.

### Creating a Custom Rule
1. Click "Add Rule".
2. Fill in:
   - Sign Type - Select from the catalog.
   - Location Type - Interior, Exterior, or Vehicle.
   - Budget Range - Select the budget tier.
   - Enabled Tiers - Choose which options to present (Good, Better, Best). You can enable 1, 2, or all 3.
   - Products - Select products for each enabled tier.
   - Tier Sign Types (optional) - Use a different sign type for AI mockup per tier.
3. Click "Save Rule".

### Example
If you want to always present only "Better" and "Best" options for Channel Letters in the $2K-5K budget range:
1. Create or copy a rule for that combination.
2. Uncheck "Good", keep "Better" and "Best" checked.
3. Set the products for Better and Best tiers.
4. Save. Now your team will only see 2 options instead of 3.

## Reference Measurements Tool

Navigate to: Sidebar > Reference Measurements

A standalone measurement tool for quick on-site estimation. Use it when you just need rough measurements of a site area from a photo without creating a full opportunity.

### How to Use
1. Click the upload area or drag a site photo onto it (JPG, PNG, or WebP).
2. Click 4 points clockwise on the photo to define the area to measure.
3. Drag points to adjust. Scroll to zoom. Alt+drag to pan.
4. Check "Add Reference Measurement" after placing all 4 points.
5. Click 2 points on a known feature (door, window, storefront width).
6. Enter the real-world dimension and select inches or feet.
7. The estimated area dimensions and square footage appear immediately.

This tool does not save data — it's purely for quick measurements.

## Working with Opportunities

As Owner Admin, you see ALL opportunities across your team on the Opportunities page.

### Opportunities List

Navigate to: Sidebar > Opportunities

Screen Layout:
- Page title: "Opportunities" with total count.
- "New Opportunity" button (top right).
- List of opportunity cards, each with:
  - Colored left border (status indicator).
  - Business name, address.
  - Sign type badge, budget range, location type.
  - Creation date.

### Creating an Opportunity
1. Click "New Opportunity".
2. Fill in the intake form:
   - Business Name (required) - The client's company.
   - Contact Name - Primary contact person.
   - Address (required) - Site address (used for sign code lookups).
   - Phone, Email - Client contact details.
   - Notes - Internal notes (only visible to your team, not in proposals).
3. Add Sign Specs:
   - Click "Add Sign Spec" for each sign you want to propose.
   - For each spec, fill in:
     - Location Type: Interior, Exterior, or Vehicle.
     - Sign Type: Choose from the catalog.
     - Budget Range: Select the budget tier.
     - Target Audience: Who the sign is for.
     - Sign Duration: Permanent or Temporary.
     - Read Distance (optional): How far away the sign needs to be readable.
     - Prompt: Creative brief for the AI. Be specific about colors, style, and materials.
4. Click "Save".

### Opportunity Detail Page

Click into any opportunity to see the full detail page.

Screen Layout:
- Top section: Business name (large), address (clickable for maps), contact info.
- Status buttons: Open, Won, Follow-up, Lost (click to change status).
- Edit and Delete buttons (top right).
- Project Notes: Internal notes area with auto-save (saves automatically as you type).
- Sign Code section (for exterior signs).
- Sign Specs section: Numbered step indicators, one tab per spec.

### Sign Spec Workflow

Each sign spec has these steps:

Step 1: Upload Assets
- Site Photo (optional but recommended): Upload a photo of the building or location.
- Logo (optional but recommended): Upload the client's logo.
- Better photos produce better AI mockups.

Step 2: Define Placement Plane
- Click 4 points on the site photo to define where the sign goes.
- Drag points to adjust position.
- Scroll to zoom. Alt+drag to pan.
- Click "Save Plane" to save.

Step 3: Reference Measurement (optional)
- After placing 4 points, check "Add Reference Measurement".
- Draw a reference line over a known dimension (e.g., a standard door is 80 inches tall).
- Enter the real-world measurement and choose inches or feet.
- The system calculates estimated sign dimensions (width x height and square footage).

Step 4: Sign Code Lookup (exterior signs)
- For exterior sign types with "Show Sign Code" enabled, there's a toggle to look up local sign regulations.
- When enabled, the AI researches municipal bylaws based on the business address.
- Results include: permit requirements, maximum sign area, height restrictions, illumination rules, setback distances.

Step 5: Generate Mockups
- Click "Generate" to create AI mockups.
- The system generates options based on your product rules (Good/Better/Best).
- Generation takes 30-90 seconds depending on complexity.
- If the AI is busy, it automatically retries up to 4 times.
- If the primary AI fails, it automatically falls back to a backup AI service.

Step 6: Review Results
- Results show side by side: "Before" on the left, "After" on the right.
- Each tier (Good, Better, Best) has its own result card.
- Download individual mockup images with the download button.
- Edit Project Notes per tier to describe the option — notes auto-save as you type.
- Click "Polish with AI" to automatically rewrite rough notes into professional proposal text.

### Exporting PDF Proposals
1. Open an opportunity that has generated mockups.
2. Click "Export PDF".
3. The system generates a professional PDF that includes:
   - Your company header: logo, name, address, phone, email, website.
   - Custom header text (if configured in Company Settings).
   - Client information: business name, address, contact details.
   - Prepared by: salesperson name and contact info.
   - Permit notice (if applicable exterior sign types).
   - Each sign spec with tier mockups (Before + After side by side).
   - Project notes per tier.
   - Custom footer text (if configured).
   - AI disclaimer on every page.
4. The PDF downloads automatically to your computer.

## Tips for Owner Admins

1. Complete onboarding first - You can't add users until setup is done.
2. Upload a high-quality logo - It appears in proposals and improves AI mockups.
3. Customize your PDF colors and header/footer - Add your license number, tagline, or terms for a polished look.
4. Set up custom product rules - Tailor the Good/Better/Best options for your business.
5. Monitor your team's opportunities - You see everything, so you can help where needed.
6. Train your team on prompts - Better prompts = better AI mockups. Encourage specific descriptions like "brushed aluminum channel letters with blue LED halo illumination" instead of "nice letters".
7. Review mockups before client presentation - Use Project Notes to add professional descriptions and use "Polish with AI" for quick refinement.
8. Use the Reference Measurements tool - Great for quick on-site estimates without creating a full opportunity.
9. Keep your subscription current - An expired subscription will block your entire team from accessing features.
10. Manage your team's access levels - Consider giving Admin role only to trusted team leads, keep others as Sales or appropriate role.
