NOTE: Full Scene Mockup (single-step — no plane points)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When no plane points are provided (user did not mark a sign placement area on
the site photo), the system falls back to a single-step "full scene" generation
that asks Gemini to render the complete installed sign scene directly, rather
than generating a sign face and compositing it. The prompt logic follows the
same template structure (glass/dimensional/flat panel) but instructs Gemini to
show the complete building with the sign installed and the surroundings fully
rendered — no compositing step is performed.

Additionally, after every two-step composite, the system runs an AI blending
pass asking Gemini to seamlessly integrate the hard-composited sign into the
building photo for photorealistic shadow, edge, and lighting integration.
